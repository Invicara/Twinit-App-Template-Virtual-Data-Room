# Managing Subsections

Subsections sit inside a Section and are the containers for your documents and links. Each subsection has a **number**, a **name**, and an optional **description**.

---

## Creating a Subsection

> **Role required:** Section Admin or Room Admin.

1. Find the section you want to add a subsection to
2. Click the **New Subsection** button at the bottom of the section card
3. In the dialog, enter:
   - **Number** — used for ordering within the section (e.g. `01.01`, `01.02`). Subsections are sorted alphabetically by number within their section.
   - **Name** — a descriptive name (e.g. "Contracts")
   - **Description** *(optional)* — additional context shown in the subsection table
4. Save the subsection

> **Note:** The New Subsection button is only shown to Section Admins and Room Admins. Section Contributors and Viewers do not see this button.

---

## Editing a Subsection

> **Role required:** Section Admin or Room Admin.

1. Click the **pencil (edit) icon** that appears next to the subsection's number
2. Update the number, name, or description
3. Save your changes

> **Note:** The edit icon is visible to all users but is only active (clickable) for Section Admins and Room Admins. Section Contributors and Viewers see a dimmed, non-clickable version.

---

## The Subsection Status Bar

Each subsection row in the table shows a **status bar** — a coloured horizontal bar that represents the breakdown of statuses across all documents and links in that subsection.

| Colour | Status |
|---|---|
| Green | Approved |
| Red | Rejected |
| Yellow/Amber | For Review |
| Grey | No status assigned |

This gives you a quick at-a-glance view of how complete or approved a subsection's content is, without needing to expand it.

---

## Expanding a Subsection

Click anywhere on the **expand arrow** (chevron) to the left of a subsection row to expand it. When expanded, you will see:

- A **document upload area** on the left *(visible to Section Contributors and Admins; hidden for Viewers)*
- The **document list** on the right
- A **link form** and **link list** below *(link form visible to Contributors and Admins; hidden for Viewers)*

See [Documents](./documents.md) and [Links](./links.md) for details on working with content inside an expanded subsection.

---

## Moving a Subsection to Trash

> **Role required:** Section Admin or Room Admin.

1. Click the **pencil (edit) icon** on the subsection
2. In the edit dialog, use the **trash / delete** option

The subsection moves to the Trash Bin along with all of its documents and links.

> **Warning:** Permanently deleting a subsection from the Trash Bin also permanently deletes all documents and links inside it.
