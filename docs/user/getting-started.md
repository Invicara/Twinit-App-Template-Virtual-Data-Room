# Getting Started

## Signing In

When you first open the Virtual Data Room you will be automatically redirected to the Twinit sign-in page. Enter your Twinit credentials and sign in.

After signing in, you will be returned to the VDR application.

---

## Selecting a Room

If your account belongs to more than one Virtual Data Room, you will see a **Room Picker** dialog after signing in.

- Use the search box to filter Rooms by name
- Click the Room you want to open

Your selection is remembered for the duration of your browser session. If you close the tab and reopen the application, you will be asked to select a Room again.

If you do not belong to any Rooms, the dialog will display:
> _"You are not yet a member of any Virtual Data Rooms. Please contact your Room administrator."_

Contact whoever administers your organisation's VDR to request access.

---

## The Application Header

The header is visible on every page and shows:
- The **Twinit logo** on the left
- The name of your currently active Room (e.g. **Virtual Data Room: My Project**)
- Your **user avatar** (your initials) and name on the right

Clicking the chevron next to your name opens a menu with the following options:

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **My Profile** | Opens Twinit account settings in the same tab |
| **Switch Room** | Returns to the Room Picker so you can open a different Room |
| **Logout** | Signs you out of the application |

---

## Session Expiry

If your session expires while you are using the application, a **Session Expired** overlay will appear over the page. Click **Sign In** to re-authenticate. Your previous work in the application (e.g. the Room you had selected) will not be lost.

---

## Navigation

The main Virtual Data Room page shows your Sections. What you can do from the toolbar depends on your role:

| Toolbar element | Who sees it |
|---|---|
| **Search bar** | All users |
| **New Section** button | Room Admins only |
| **Hamburger menu (☰)** | Room Admins only |

The hamburger menu (visible to Room Admins) provides navigation to:
- **Manage Users** — invite users and view group membership
- **View Trash Bin** — restore or permanently delete trashed items

If you are a Section Admin, Section Contributor, or Section Viewer, you will see only the search bar in the toolbar. All your actions take place within the section cards below.

See [Access & Roles](./access-and-roles.md) for a full breakdown of what each role can do.
